CHI CASH WHITE PAPER
A Demurrage-Based Art Currency for Community Barter and Regenerative Circulation
By Chisum Burton
1. Abstract
Modern fiat and cryptocurrency systems reward hoarding, slow circulation, and concentrate wealth in the hands of those who store value rather than those who create it. Chi Cash proposes a hybrid physical-digital currency designed to reverse this dynamic through demurrage, velocity mining, and art-backed physical notes.

Each Chi Coin unit is a unique 1cc serial number, 12 digits, that circulates, expires, and regenerates value through real economic activity. Physical notes contain bundles of these serials and feature original artwork from invited artists. As Chi Cash moves through communities, it “blooms,” creating new Chi Coins, while expired units flow back into the network to be mined by another user. 
Chi Cash is not a speculative asset. It is a community engine, a barter facilitator, and a living economic organism designed to reward participation, generosity, and local trade.

Physical art notes

Community-visible savings plans
The goal is simple:
To create a global barter-friendly network that rewards circulation, supports local communities, and allows real world actions to mine a crypto coin. 
Chi Barter Network is reserve-backed, transparent, and designed to be both collectible and functional.
3. System Overview
Chi Coins exists in two forms:
What is a Chi Coin? It has a biology cycle, it has varying value states. It can be minted by bartering for CC. 
The Biology Cycle of Chi Coin phases- Fresh, Mass, Sprout, Bloom, and Seed. 
The value states- Fresh 100%, Mass 95%, Seed 90% of Mass, Bloom 100% and mints new Fresh CC. Lastly Seed, this interesting phase deletes the coin's serial number allowing it to be reminted by another user but it remains in a user's wallet as a .8 of a CC coin. When a user gains .2 more fractional, a new coin is automatically minted from the two fractional pieces. 
The purpose of Mass and Sprout- these small percentages are fees that help Chi Barter Network run. 
The purpose of Bloom- to reward real world transactions with more buying power. 
The purpose of Seed- to prevent hyper inflation of CC. 

A. Physical Notes
Printed on high-quality paper with original artwork. Each note contains a fixed number of 1cc serial units:
Notes contain no numbers in the artwork. A 12-digit serial number and barcode are stamped along the edge. A note must be scanned via QR to observe it's balance. This paper form is expected to be mainly useful in regions that lack Smartphones and have only access to the system via paper real form barter. 
B. Digital Units
Each 1cc serial exists digitally with:
A unique 12 digit ID
A value state- 100, 95, .90% of 95, 100, and .80%
A status- Active and Pending.  
A last-four-wallet transaction history- This prevents, flags, and tracks Boomerang and Ping Pong Transactions
An expiration timer- Once a coin reaches 2 months of age in a wallet without movement and not in a Savings Plan, it is deleted from the ledger. This is what we call demurrage.  
A phase state- Fresh, Mass, Sprout, Bloom, and Seed. At Bloom new coins are minted in the recipient's wallet. 

Digital wallets automatically manage serials using autopay logic.
4. CC Serial States

Each CC serial has two independent state systems:
A. Status
Active – in circulation
Pending – locked in an offline transaction window or in Savings Plan. 

B. Value State
Each serial progresses through:
1. Fresh – 100% value
2. Mass – 95% value (5% fee to CHIHQ001)
3. Sprout – 90% value of Mass State (another 5% fee to CHIHQ001)
4. Bloom – 100% value + mints a new Fresh serial
5. Seed – .80% a fractional CC. 

This cycle repeats indefinitely.

5. Velocity Mining (Bloom)
When a CC serial reaches Bloom, it:
Remains Bloom until its next transaction.
Mints a new 1CC Fresh coin into the recipient’s wallet.
Then transitions to Seed on its next transaction.

Bloom rewards movement, not storage.
This encourages:
Local trade
Entrepreneurship
Community participation
Circulation over hoarding

6. Demurrage & Expiration
Every serial has a 2-month lifespan after activation.
If not spent before expiration:
Expired CC is not destroyed — it is recycled into the network waiting to be minted again by an active seller.

7. Anti-Boomerang and Ping Pong Rules
To prevent laundering and artificial velocity, each serial tracks its last four wallets.
A transaction is rejected if:
The recipient appears in the last two wallets
The pattern forms A ? B ? C ? B
The pattern forms A ? B ? A
This ensures organic circulation.

8. Autopay Logic
Digital wallets automatically choose which serials to spend:
1. Soonest to expire first
2. Lowest value state first (Seed -- Sprout -- Mass -- Fresh -- Bloom)
3. Avoid boomerang risk

Users cannot manually select digital serials. 
Exception: Physical Notes
When using paper notes, users may choose which note to hand over.
This gives physical real world paper art notes a small strategic advantage for Bloom. 

9. Offline Transactions
If no internet is available:
Buyer and seller exchange a signed offline transaction
The involved serials enter Pending state
Pending serials cannot expire
One party must upload the transaction within 24 hours
If uploaded transaction completes
If not serials revert to previous wallet
This preserves barter even without connectivity.
